# Evaluation status

The review procedure in `README.md` was evaluated as an addition to the short documentation rule using 36 fixed drafts and three independent revisions per instruction variant.

**Decision: retain the short rule as the default; the detailed procedure remains a candidate.** On held-out comparisons against the short rule, the candidate won 4 times, lost once, and tied 66 times, with one invalid judgment. It did not meet the predeclared adoption threshold.

The original-guideline baseline was already strong. The experiment did not establish that either additional instruction improves quality generally; the short rule sometimes prompted unnecessary rewriting.

See the [full report](../../../../evals/documenting/results/full-openai55-v1/report.md) for models, methods, limitations, and reproducible results. These findings concern supplied-draft finalization, not initial generation or automatic skill loading.
